Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version: this website keeps nothing about you, and a farm's records belong to the farm. The rest of this page says exactly what that means.

1. Who we are

Plaashek builds and runs farm software for South African farms. Plaashek is owned by Dawie Pieterse, who is also its Information Officer under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Write to info@plaashek.co.za or phone +27 83 456 5646.

2. This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. plaashek.co.za sets no cookies and loads nothing from an advertising or tracking company. Its font and pictures come from the site itself. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
  • Nothing you tick or type is sent to us by the page. The "What are you interested in?" boxes only write a WhatsApp message or an email for you. It goes nowhere until you send it yourself.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the address (IP) of each request in its server logs for security. We do not see those logs.

3. When you contact us

If you send a WhatsApp message, an email or phone us, we receive your name, your number or address, and what you tell us about your farm. We use it to answer you and to prepare a quote, and for nothing else. WhatsApp is a service of Meta and handles messages under its own privacy policy. If nothing comes of the enquiry, ask us and we delete it.

4. Farms that use Plaashek's apps

The farm's records are the farm's. Each farm has its own installation and its own database, on a server in South Africa. One farm's records are never mixed with another's. For the personal information a farm keeps in the apps (its workers' names, attendance and pay, for example), the farm is the responsible party under POPIA and Plaashek is its operator: we process it only to provide the service, on the farm's instruction.

What Plaashek itself keeps about a farm: the farm's name, town and province, the owner's name, cell number and email, the billing name and address, the apps it uses, its quotes and invoices, and a count of how many people and phones use the installation (numbers only, no names).

Who at Plaashek can see a farm's records: Plaashek staff, only to set up, support and fix the farm's installation. Each support sign-in is logged on the farm with the staff member's name. Pay and identity details are open to staff only while the owner switches that on. Workers' identity, bank and tax numbers are stored encrypted.

5. Others who help us provide the service

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. These providers process information for us, and only for the purpose shown:

  • Afrihost (South Africa): hosts the farms' installations and our email.
  • Backblaze: holds the nightly backups. They are encrypted before they leave the server, and may be stored outside South Africa.
  • Open-Meteo: receives the farm's position to return its weather and forecast. No person's details.
  • Anthropic (Claude), in the United States: only once the owner switches it on. Plaasjapie sends the farm's figures needed for the question asked (a worker by number, never by name), and Diesel's invoice reading sends the photo of the invoice. Plaashek's own office assistant uses farms' contact details and billing figures. Not used to train the model.
  • OpenAI, in the United States: a spoken field note is sent to be written out as text. Not used to train the model.
  • EskomSePush: receives the farm's load-shedding area, where the farm uses that. No person's details.
  • ClickUp: our support list. A problem we log may include a screenshot of the screen it happened on.

Where one of these is outside South Africa, we send only what the task needs, under that provider's data-protection terms, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • A farm's records: for as long as the farm uses Plaashek. The owner can export them at any time.
  • When a farm leaves: the owner can still sign in and download everything for 12 months. Then the installation and its backups are removed.
  • Quotes, invoices and payment records: five years, as tax law requires.
  • Enquiries that did not become a quote: until you ask us to delete them, and at most two years.

7. How we protect it

Every page is served over HTTPS. Plaashek staff sign in with two-factor authentication. Changes to a farm's settings and to our own records are written to an audit log. Backups are encrypted. A phone only reaches a farm's records after the farm's office has paired it. If personal information is ever compromised, we tell the farms affected and the Information Regulator, as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. Write to info@plaashek.co.za. If you work on a farm that uses Plaashek, ask the farm first: the records are the farm's, and we help the farm to answer you.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, inforegulator.org.za.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date. Farms that use Plaashek are told of any change that affects their records.
