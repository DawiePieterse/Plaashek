4. Price and payment

  • Every farm's price is its own quote: a monthly amount per app and for hosting, and a once-off setup fee. Accepting the quote starts the service.

5. Late payment

We send reminders for an overdue invoice. At 30 days overdue the account goes into a grace period. At 60 days overdue the paid apps become read-only: your records can still be read and exported, but nothing new is saved until the account is paid. Everything is switched back on as soon as it is.

The apps are not tax, legal or agronomic advice.
